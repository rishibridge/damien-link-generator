How to Use the Damien Center Link, QR Code & PDF Generator

Step-by-step instructions for generating Indiana employer enrollment links, QR codes, and customized program PDFs.

For Brokers & Direct Employer Setup (Generating Links, QR Codes & PDFs)

  1. Enter Business / Employer Name: Type the employer name (e.g., LoCascio Hadden & Dennis or any new business name) and click Generate URL & QR Code. Your broker code and first-month coupon code are applied automatically.
  2. Paste into Email or PowerPoint: Click Copy URL for Email / PPT to copy the enrollment link, or click Copy QR Image to Clipboard and press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on Mac) directly inside your email or slide deck.
  3. Download Ready-to-Share Program PDFs: Click Download 1-Page Flyer PDF (Page 4 Employee Enrollment Flyer) or Download Full 4-Page Packet PDF (Program Overview, FAQs + Enrollment Flyer). Both PDFs are automatically customized with your employer's clickable enrollment link and QR code.
  4. Quick Access Table: Every generated business link appears in the table at the bottom of the page with 1-click Copy URL, Download QR, 1-Page PDF, and 4-Page PDF buttons.

For Damien Center Admins (Managing Brokers, Coupons & Direct Links)

  1. Share Self-Service Links with Brokers: In 1. Broker & Coupon Setup, click Copy Broker Link next to any broker (e.g., /?broker=lhd or /?broker=epic) and send it to that broker. Brokers only see their own portal and their own employer links — no sign-in required for them.
  2. Direct Employers (No Broker): Select Damien Center (Direct — No Broker) in the dropdown (or use /?broker=damien) to generate links for direct employers using coupon firstmonth.
  3. Add a New Broker or Update a Coupon: Enter a new Broker Name and their Already-Created Coupon Code and click + Add / Save Broker, or click Edit Coupon on any existing row to update its coupon code.
