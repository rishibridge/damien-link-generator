Damien Center — End-to-End Admin Process & Portal Guide

Complete operational workflow for setting up benefit brokers, generating employer links & QR codes, and distributing customized program PDFs.

How the Damien Center Indiana Employer Program Works: Every participating Indiana employer receives a unique enrollment URL (https://app.helloalpha.com/damien-health?ref=<EmployerCode>&coupon=<CouponCode>), a matching scannable QR Code, and a customized 2027 Program PDF Flyer & 4-Page Packet. The ?ref= parameter tracks enrollments back to the specific employer and broker, while the &coupon= parameter automatically reduces the employee's first-month membership to $1 ($49/month thereafter).
Process 1

Delegating to a Benefit Broker (Self-Service Setup)

Use 1. Broker & Coupon Setup (left panel) when partnering with a benefit brokerage (such as LHD, Epic, NFP, Conner, or McGohan Brabender) so they can generate their own employer links:

  1. Confirm the Coupon Code Exists: Ensure the broker's first-month coupon code (e.g., firstmonthLHD, firstmonthconner, firstmonthmb, or firstmonth) has already been created in Hello Alpha.
  2. Add or Update the Broker: Enter the Broker Name and their Already-Created Coupon Code in Panel 1 and click + Add / Save Broker (or click Edit Coupon on an existing row to change a coupon).
  3. Send the Dedicated Broker Portal Link: In the Broker table, click Copy Broker Link (e.g., /?broker=lhd or /?broker=epic) and email that link to your contact at the brokerage.
  4. Strict Broker Isolation: When a broker opens their dedicated link, they do not need a password, they cannot see the Admin Setup panel, and they can only view and generate employer links belonging to their own brokerage.
Process 2

Generating Direct Employer Links (Or Generating for a Broker)

Use 2. Generate Employer URL & QR Code (right panel) whenever Damien Center is onboarding a direct employer (no broker) or generating materials on a broker's behalf:

  1. Select Broker or Direct: Choose Damien Center (Direct — No Broker) for direct employers (automatically applies coupon firstmonth), or select a specific Broker from the dropdown.
  2. Enter the Business / Employer Name: Type the business name (e.g., Martin Farms, Harsha Behavioral Health, or any new Indiana employer) and click Generate URL & QR Code.
    All 66 pre-loaded businesses across Damien Direct, Epic, LHD, NFP, Conner, and MGB automatically resolve to their exact official ?ref= code (e.g., LoCascio Hadden & Dennis → LCHD). Any new business name automatically generates a standardized ?ref= code.
  3. Share the Customized PDFs, QR Code, or Link: Use the output panel (or any row in the bottom table) to download the 1-Page Flyer PDF, Full 4-Page Packet PDF, or copy the link and QR image into an email or PowerPoint deck.
Process 3

Choosing Between PDF Flyer, 4-Page Packet, QR PNG & URL

Every generated employer entry provides four ready-to-use formats depending on who you are sending materials to:

  • 1-Page Flyer PDF (Employee Distribution): Exports Page 4 of the 2027 DC Weight Management template with the employer's scannable QR code stamped under "Scan to enroll" and their clickable URL underlined inside the magenta "Enroll at:" banner. Ideal for HR to email or print for employees.
  • Full 4-Page Packet PDF (HR & Leadership Packet): Exports all 4 pages: Page 1 (2027 Program Pricing & Overview), Page 2 (Program FAQs), Page 3 (Combined 1-Page Summary + FAQ), and Page 4 (the customized Employee Enrollment Flyer with the employer's QR code and clickable link).
  • Copy URL / Copy QR Image (Custom Emails & Slides): Click Copy URL for Email / PPT or Copy QR Image to Clipboard (then press Ctrl+V / Cmd+V in Outlook, Gmail, or PowerPoint) when building custom presentations.
Welcome to Your Dedicated Broker Self-Service Portal: This portal lets your brokerage generate customized enrollment links, scannable QR codes, and ready-to-share PDF flyers for any Indiana employer client enrolling in the Damien Center Weight Management Program. Every link you generate automatically embeds your brokerage's assigned first-month coupon (firstmonth — reducing the employee's first month to $1) and a unique employer ?ref= code so enrollments are attributed to your client.
Step 1

Generate Your Client's Unique Enrollment Link & QR Code

Setting up a new or existing employer client takes less than 5 seconds:

  1. Enter the Business / Employer Name: In the generator box on this page, type your client's business name (e.g., Bose McKinney & Evans, Resultant, or any new employer client).
  2. Click "Generate URL & QR Code": The portal automatically formats the employer's ?ref= code, attaches your brokerage's coupon code, generates a scannable QR code, and saves the employer to your brokerage's table at the bottom of the page.
  3. Automatic Code Matching: No employee ever needs to type a promo code manually — when an employee clicks the generated link or scans the QR code, their $1 first-month discount and employer attribution are applied automatically.
Step 2

Download Customized PDF Flyers & Employer Packets

You do not need to manually edit PDFs or paste QR codes into flyers — the portal generates customized PDFs on the fly for every employer:

  • Download 1-Page Flyer PDF (For Employees): Click Download 1-Page Flyer PDF (With Link & QR) (or 1-Page PDF in the table). This produces the 1-page Employee Enrollment Flyer with your client's custom QR code stamped under "Scan to enroll" and their clickable URL inside the magenta "Enroll at:" banner. Send this directly to the employer's HR team to share with employees.
  • Download Full 4-Page Packet PDF (For HR / Benefits Decision-Makers): Click Download Full 4-Page Packet PDF (With Link & QR) (or 4-Page PDF in the table). This produces the complete 4-page 2027 Program Packet:
    • Page 1: 2027 Program Pricing & What's Included ($49/mo membership + $29/$99 Wegovy)
    • Page 2: Frequently Asked Questions (7 Employer & Employee FAQs)
    • Page 3: Combined 1-Page Program Overview & FAQ Summary
    • Page 4: Customized Employee Enrollment Flyer with your client's QR code & clickable link
Step 3

Embed Links & QR Codes in Open-Enrollment Emails or Decks

If you are building a custom open-enrollment presentation, benefits guide, or email blast for an employer:

  1. Copy the Enrollment Link: Click Copy URL for Email / PPT to copy the full https://app.helloalpha.com/damien-health?ref=... link to your clipboard.
  2. Paste the QR Code Directly into Email or PowerPoint: Click Copy QR Image to Clipboard, switch to your email or PowerPoint slide, and press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on Mac) to paste the crisp QR image directly.
  3. Download Standalone QR PNG: Click Download QR PNG File (or Download QR in the table) if your design team needs a high-resolution 600 DPI .png image file.
  4. Re-Download Anytime: All employer links generated by your brokerage remain in the table at the bottom of this page so your team can re-copy links or download PDFs anytime.
